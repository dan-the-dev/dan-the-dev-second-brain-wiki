## Domande di candidatura

**1. Why Duvo / great fit**
> I've spent the last year building AI workflows into my daily practice — not experimentally, but as part of how I actually ship. I use Claude Code as a pair programming partner, not autocomplete: it helps me hold quality standards even when I'm tired or under pressure. What Duvo is building — agents that finish the job, not just assist — is exactly the problem space I think about. The TypeScript-first stack is home ground. The culture (ship weekly, iterate forward, direct feedback, AI budget with no ceiling) reads like XP written in different words. I don't want to work *near* AI systems. I want to build them, own them end-to-end, and measure them against real customer outcomes. That's what drew me to Duvo specifically.

**2. Excellence — work + football**
> Two areas, genuinely linked. At work: I spent years frustrated that quality and speed felt like a tradeoff. Then I found XP — extreme programming, lean, devops — and discovered they're not. TDD, continuous deployment, short feedback loops: once you build them into the workflow, you don't negotiate quality with the business, it's just there. That pursuit has been a decade-long obsession. Football is the other one. I've played in amateur teams my whole life and I'm a deep student of the game — tactics, positioning, how a team creates and holds shape under pressure. The parallels are obvious: system design, collective ownership, what happens when one person breaks the pattern. Both have taught me that excellence is a practice, not a talent.

**3. Anything else**
> One thing I've learned building AI workflows: agents are most valuable not when they do things I can't do, but when they hold standards I would otherwise drop under pressure. I've built personal coding agents that enforce review, test coverage, and deployment gates — because I know exactly where corners get cut when the deadline moves. Outside of code, I've been working on [Learn Agile Practices — link], sharing what I've learned about XP, lean, and technical excellence. Teaching forces a clarity I can't fake, and it keeps me honest about the gap between what I preach and what I actually ship.
