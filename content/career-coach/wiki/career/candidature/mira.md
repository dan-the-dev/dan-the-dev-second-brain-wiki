# Mira (Your Own Circle) — Founding CTO / Technical Co-Founder

https://yourowncircle.com

## L'azienda

**Prodotto**: Mira — People Operating System per il settore hospitality
**Cosa fa**: Piattaforma employee experience per hotel group: operazioni (SOP, checklist, rote, training), wellbeing (feedback anonimo, mood, recognition), HR & compliance (policy, e-signature, GDPR audit trail), performance (KPI e goal), AI assistant per staff frontline/manager/HR/executive
**Architettura**: Multi-tenant from the ground up (org → property → department → team)
**Stage**: Prodotto funzionante in produzione, moving to pilot launch con hotel reali
**Location**: UK / Remote EU (CET)
**Contatto**: Cristiano (founder, business side)

## Il ruolo

**Titolo**: Founding CTO / Technical Co-Founder
**Natura**: Co-founder tecnico — hands-on + leadership + ownership totale della direzione tecnica
**Compensazione**: Equity-based (vesting) — part-time fino al pilot stage, full-time dopo
**Team**: 2-3 developer da guidare

### Responsabilità
- Ownership completa della strategia tecnica e architettura
- Hands-on: codice in produzione ogni giorno
- Lead team 2-3 developer: code review, unblocking, mentoring
- MVP → demo-ready → production launch con pilot hotels
- Multi-tenant isolation, security, GDPR, AI safety, reliability
- Partner del founding team su prodotto e fundraising

## Stack tecnico

- **Backend**: Node.js / NestJS (modular monolith) ✅
- **Frontend**: React (Vite), TypeScript, mobile-first ✅
- **Database**: PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM ✅
- **AI**: LLM assistant + RAG su SOP/data aziendali, tenant-scoped ✅
- **Infra**: AWS (CDK / ECS Fargate), CI/CD ⚠️ (IaC da gestire)
